## Method

Dual-Slot replaces serial loop execution with shared-parameter latent and prediction slots. The prediction slot combines the current representation with the previous token's deep latent state; in parallel, the latent slot computes a state for the next decoding step. This reduces the serial dependency between loop invocations.

## Results

The method is evaluated on Qwen-style dense and MoE architectures. At 1B dense parameters, PPL decreases by 0.25 and five-shot downstream average accuracy improves by about 2.3 percentage points relative to a vanilla Transformer. With matched SFT, zero-shot GSM8K accuracy improves by approximately 5.8 percentage points. Dense and MoE models retain about 93–97% and 98–100% of baseline decoding throughput, respectively.
