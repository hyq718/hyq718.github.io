## Method

P2N introduces cross-token deep-representation reuse: the previous token's core output is added to the current token's core input, allowing current computation to build on already-completed deep computation. Jacobi iterations over a local core preserve token-parallel training. During autoregressive decoding, the previous state is already available, so the feedback needs only one vector addition per step and introduces no additional parameters.

## Results

The method is evaluated on Qwen-style dense and MoE architectures. At 1.5B dense parameters, Pile perplexity decreases by 0.2 and general downstream average accuracy improves by approximately 2 percentage points. With matched supervised fine-tuning, GSM8K accuracy improves by about 11 percentage points while decoding latency remains nearly unchanged.
