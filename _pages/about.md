## About

I am an undergraduate at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)**, researching deep learning and large language model pretraining architectures. Since June 2026, I have been working on **LLM pretraining and architecture exploration at IQuest**.

My research asks how to increase a language model's **effective computational depth** while keeping training and decoding efficient. I study how to allocate computation across tokens, reuse representations across layers and tokens, and parallelize recurrent computation.

## Research Interests

- **Pretraining architectures:** looped Transformers, recurrent models, and effective depth.
- **Latent reasoning and adaptive computation:** token-level latent thoughts and learned halting.
- **Efficient information flow:** cross-layer value mixing, residual connections, and parallel decoding.
